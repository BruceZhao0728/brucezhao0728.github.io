# Personal Website

This glossary names the people and content formats used across Zichen Zhao's personal website.

## Language

**Visitor**:
Anyone interested in learning about Zichen Zhao's work or life, including collaborators, peers, recruiters, and friends.
_Avoid_: Research audience

**Language edition**:
The visitor-selected English or Chinese presentation of page content, navigation, and blog summaries.
_Avoid_: Mixed-language page

**Original-language article**:
A blog article whose body remains in the language in which it was written, regardless of the selected language edition.
_Avoid_: Untranslated article
